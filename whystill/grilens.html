GRI-Lens

AI-Powered Sustainability Report Gap Analysis

An intelligent document analysis platform designed to assist the assessment of sustainability reports against GRI disclosures.

AI AssessmentEvidence TracingGap IdentificationHuman Review

THE CHALLENGE

Hundreds of pages. Detailed requirements. Evidence everywhere.

Sustainability reports can contain hundreds of pages, while evaluating disclosure completeness requires careful examination of specific requirements and supporting evidence.

THE SOLUTION

Structured assessment with traceable evidence.

GRI-Lens analyzes uploaded PDF sustainability reports, assesses selected GRI disclosures on a structured 0–5 scale, and links each result to supporting evidence from the original report.

INSIDE GRI-LENS

From Documents to Actionable Insights.

GRI-Lens goes beyond simple document summarization.

  • Closest EvidencePresent the most relevant supporting evidence for each disclosure.
  • Required ElementsIdentify which disclosure requirements are found, partial, or missing.
  • Suggested FixProvide practical recommendations to improve incomplete disclosures.
  • Human ReviewAllow assessors to review evidence, adjust scores, and provide feedback.
  • Version ComparisonTrack improvements across multiple analysis versions.
  • Excel ExportExport results for further analysis and reporting.
